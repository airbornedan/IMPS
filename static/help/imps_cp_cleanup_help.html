Control Panel: Cleanup

This tab links to tools for finding and resolving data that's fallen out of sync, usually as a side effect of deleting a box or replacing an item's photo:

  • Show orphaned items – items whose assigned box number no longer exists. Lets you move each one into a valid box or delete it.
  • Show unlinked photos – photo files on disk that are no longer linked to any item. Lets you delete them individually or all at once to reclaim disk space.

Neither tool changes anything until you take an action on a specific item or file — visiting these pages is safe and just shows you what's currently out of sync.
